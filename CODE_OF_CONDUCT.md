# Code of conduct

motel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Read the full text at that address. This file is the short form.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project without harassment, whatever their age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and say what you mean plainly.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Keep the discussion on the project.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for this policy. They can edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests
that break it, and can restrict or ban a person who does so. The Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe how they respond.

## How do I report a problem?

Contact the maintainers through GitHub. For something that should stay private, open an issue that says only that you
want to report a conduct problem, with no details, and a maintainer will arrange a private channel. All reports are
handled in confidence.

This code applies in all project spaces, and when a person represents the project in public.
